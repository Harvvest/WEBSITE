A STRUCTURED LEARNING JOURNEY

Understand the why.
Practise the how.

From market foundations to practical application, build your skills one concept at a time.

01

Market Foundations

Start with the language of the market.

Market segments, participants, brokers and how the market works.

02

Technical Analysis

Learn to read price with context.

OHLC, candlesticks, swing theory, trends, support & resistance, breakouts, retests and backtesting.

03

Risk & Psychology

Put a process behind every decision.

Capital management, probability, risk–reward, position sizing and emotional discipline.

04

Futures & Options

Understand the instruments before using them.

Derivatives, futures and options, option chains, Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega.

05

Advanced Learning

Look beyond the chart.

Specialized strategies, business and financial analysis, valuation and portfolio construction.

06

Live Market Practice

Turn concepts into guided practice.

Order placement, execution, sector monitoring, stop losses, trade journals and discussion.

Curriculum depth varies by program.
